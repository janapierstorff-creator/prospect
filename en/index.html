The challenge

Young athletes
train like pros.
But do they eat like pros?

Intense training, growth and a packed schedule: their energy and nutrient needs are high – but everyday eating often can't keep up. The fueling gap shows: less energy, less focus, less progress.

  • High energy needs

    Growth plus intense training.

  • Packed schedule

    School, training, friends.

  • Not at full power

    The skills are there – the energy isn't.
