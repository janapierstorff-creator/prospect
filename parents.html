FAQ

Good to know.

What age is ProspectFuel for?

For young athletes aged 12 to 18 who train regularly and want to keep developing in their sport.

Is ProspectFuel only for basketball?

No. We're starting with basketball, but we're building ProspectFuel for young athletes across different sports.

Does my child need special products?

No. Everyday food is the foundation. Complementary fueling products around training are planned for later.

Is ProspectFuel designed for teenagers?

Yes. We're building ProspectFuel specifically for young athletes and their needs during growth and training – together with sports nutrition experts.

What happens after I sign up?

You'll join the waitlist for our Berlin launch. We'll let you know as soon as we start and how you can take part.
